Business Programs

Explore the programs offered through the UHMC Business Department.

Business Administration

Certificates of Competence · Certificate of Achievement · Associate in Applied Science (AAS)

Build skills in entrepreneurship, accounting, and management to grow a business career or start your own small business. The AAS can transfer to bachelor’s programs at UHMC or UH West Oʻahu.

Career paths: business owner, manager, retail, sales, and marketing

Hospitality & Tourism

Associate in Applied Science (AAS)

Prepare for leadership roles in hospitality, one of Hawaiʻi’s largest industries.

Highlight: accredited by the Accreditation Commission for Programs in Hospitality Administration (ACPHA)

Accounting

Associate in Applied Science (AAS) · Certificate of Achievement (CA)

Every business needs accounting. Train for careers with small businesses, hotels, service industries, and other organizations on Maui and beyond.

Next step: with further education, graduates can advance to accountant or auditor roles

Applied Business & Information Technology (ABIT)

Bachelor of Applied Science (BAS)

A hybrid bachelor’s degree combining business, entrepreneurship, and technology skills, with online and hybrid options.

Also offered: certificates in data science and cybersecurity
